# Retrieval uses the configured score filters

Chunking creates passages with a 2,000-character body cap. Search returns at most 10 of them,
keeping only cosine similarities at or above both `min_score = 0.25` and
`best_score - score_margin`, with `score_margin = 0.20`.

If even the best score is below 0.25, no passages are returned. The floor filters weak hits;
it is not calibrated confidence and cannot reject every irrelevant query. Search never syncs.
